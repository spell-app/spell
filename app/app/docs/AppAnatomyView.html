AppAnatomyView Anatomy: the running app pulled apart into layers, in 3D, one for each view it draws.

Anatomy shows how a running app is put together. Its switch is beside Inspect app in the top bar, or View › Anatomy (⌥⌘A). The app tilts back and comes apart into layers: the app at the bottom, each view it draws a layer above the view that holds it, each with a tag naming its type.

Written for: anyone changing the spell app's editor, or reading its code A picture, not live: it needs the whole editor round it

Solitaire, pulled apart in the app pane:

A picture, taken by yarn site:pictures in spell-app.

  • Drag to turn it, scroll to zoom; Front looks straight at it again.
  • Spacing pulls the layers apart.
  • A click on a layer selects it, as a click on the app does: the Views tree, the properties and the code follow along. A double-click, or F, shows one layer and what's inside it alone; Esc goes back.
  • Views | Outlines: Views shows the app itself on its layers, Outlines draws each layer as an empty frame. The full-window button lifts the pane over the editor.
  • The app keeps running: a card moved, or a live edit, shows on its layer. Hold ⌥ to play it.
  • Turned off, every style it set on the app is put back as it was. A view whose look cuts off what's inside it comes apart in one piece, its tag saying so.
