<spell-reference-page> A reference page, made from its source, whose words you make clearer with an option-click.

A place's reference, drawn from its description each time it's shown: its lists by tag, each with its fields, what it can change and how trying it went. Your words about each thing are kept in the place's Spell file, and an option-click makes them clearer.

Written for: anyone showing what a data source holds, or building another kind of reference page Live: the real element, from the app's element build this page loads

Live, from /element/spell.pack.js (yarn build:element in spell-app), which this set's pages load.

  • src names a place's description, as spell place ingest wrote it. Its words come from the Spell file beside it.
  • Your words are -- lines in that file: the place's own under its line, a type's or a field's on the first line inside it, which Spell already reads as its description.
  • Each time spell place update looks again, your words stay with their line. A line that's gone takes its words with it, and the update says so. What it finds new, or can't find, gets a dated line: -- NEW as of 2026-11-18.
  • With editable, an option-click on something opens its words. The page around it saves them, through spell-reference-edit: the editor's place reference does.
  • Each part is a tag of its own, so a page can style it: <spell-reference-section>, <spell-reference-item>, <spell-reference-state>.
  • Its labels are in the reader's language; your words are in the file's.

From its vocabulary, by yarn site:data in spell-app.
