Chrome The menus and toolbars around the panes: bars, sections, springs, "..." menus.

The pieces every toolbar in the editor is built from: the bar across the top of each page, the header on top of each pane, the left, middle and right sections inside them, and the "..." menus. The theme picked dresses them all, as it does the rest of the editor.

Written for: anyone adding to, or changing, the spell app's toolbars A picture, not live: the editor page's top bar, an AppMenu

The editor page's AppMenu, in three sections: the project's menu (Spell's hat, its name) and File · Edit · View · Run · Help on the left; Run | Edit in the middle; on the right, one group of icons, their glyphs 12px apart: in Edit, Inspect app and the Inspector's menu, a thin line, then Restart, the theme menu and your avatar.

A picture, taken by yarn site:pictures in spell-app.

The menus themselves are MenuBar.tsx's: each a <BarMenu>, its items Actions drawn as <ui-action>s with their keys. A pane's own toolbar is a PanelMenu, the pane's header.

The editor page's top bar, above: an AppMenu of three Submenus:

SpellEditor.tsx · the editor page's top bar · 21 lines TreeViewer.tsx · a pane's toolbar · 5 lines
  • The bars: <AppMenu>, joined to the top of the window, is Spell UI's window bar, a <ui-menu appearance="header">; <PanelMenu>, on top of a pane, is a plain menu, which the theme shapes (EditorTheme.css). Each holds <ui-item>s.
  • <Submenu>: a left, middle or right section, each at least a third of the bar, so the three line up. With spring, a <Spring> pushes it away from its neighbours.
  • <Spring>: an invisible item that takes up the room left over.
  • <MenuHeader>: a pane's name on its bar, "App" or "Spell Tree".
  • <MoreMenu>: a "..." dropdown of Actions. Choosing one clicks it, so an action works the same in a menu or on a bar.
    • Same-titled items need their own values.
    • stub: a dimmed "..." that does nothing yet.
  • The project's actions are its menu's (<ProjectBarMenu>): New, Duplicate, Reset to Saved, Rename, Delete, Project Settings, the theme, More Examples and Home. The file's actions are the File menu's.
  • Each piece's host takes no room of its own (display: contents): style the boxes inside through their parts, never the host.
