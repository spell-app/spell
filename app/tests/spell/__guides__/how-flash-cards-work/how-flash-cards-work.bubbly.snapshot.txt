==== /HowFlashCardsWork.spell
⟪# How Flash Cards work⟫
⟦// The Flash Cards example is a deck of Spanish words to learn:⟧
⟦// the word on the front, what it means on the back.  Click the card to turn it over.⟧
⟦// Two ideas do the work:⟧
⟦// - a card is a TWO SIDED VIEW:  it knows which way up it is, and turns over in 3D to show that side⟧
⟦// - the quiz deals the next card at random from the deck⟧

⟦// This guide keeps those two, and leaves out the score.  Open Flash Cards, in the Examples, for the whole quiz.⟧

⟪## A card:  a word on the front, what it means on the back⟫
⟦// `template="two sided view"` makes the card one of Spell's built-in two sided views, as Solitaire's cards are.⟧
⟦// It brings, with no code of its own:⟧
⟦// - which way it faces, up or down, and `it is face up`, `it is face down`⟧
⟦// - `turn it face up`, `turn it face down`, `turn it over`⟧
⟦// - a drawing of both its sides, back to back, which shows its front face up and its back face down, and turns over in 3D⟧

⟦// So the card only says what each side looks like:  its `to draw its front` and its `to draw its back`.⟧
⟦// - `clickable` shows a pointer over it, and `on click` says what a click does.⟧
⟦// - Each side is a Spell UI `<segment>`:  a raised panel, blue for the word and green for what it means.⟧

[thing create_type L0]
| a «"flash card"» is a **clickable two sided view**
|   [property define_property_has L1]
|   | its «"word"» is **text**
|   [property define_property_has L1]
|   | its «"meaning"» is **text**

|   [action on L1]
|   | on click
|   |   turn it over    ‾value(it)

|   [action draw_it L1]
|   | to **draw** its **front**
|   |   <⟨segment⟩ appearance=«"raised"» color=«"blue"» align=«"center"» padding=«"lg"»>
|   |     <⟨text⟩ size=«"huge"» bold>{its **word**}</⟨text⟩>    code({its word})
|   |   </⟨segment⟩>
|   [action draw_it L1]
|   | to **draw** its **back**
|   |   <⟨segment⟩ appearance=«"raised"» color=«"green"» align=«"center"» padding=«"lg"»>
|   |     <⟨text⟩ size=«"huge"» bold>{its **meaning**}</⟨text⟩>    code({its meaning})
|   |   </⟨segment⟩>

[thing create_list_type L0]
| a «"deck"» is a **list of flash cards**
|   ⟦// The cards to learn⟧

⟪## The quiz:  the deck, and the card showing⟫
⟦// `deal a card` picks a card at random, never the one showing now, and turns it word up.⟧

[thing create_one_of_a_kind L0]
| «quiz» is an **app**
|   [property define_property_has L1]
|   | «"deck"» is **a new deck**
|   [property define_property_has L1]
|   | its «"card"» is a **flash card**

|   [action to_do_something L1]
|   | to «deal a card»
|   |   set **others** to **cards** in the **deck** where it is not **quiz's card**    ‾value(others) ‾value(cards in the deck where it is not quiz's card)
|   |   set the **card** to a random **card** in **others**    ‾value(the card) ‾value(a random card in others)
|   |   turn the **card** face up    ‾value(the card)

|   [action to_do_something L1]
|   | to **draw** it
|   |   <⟨container⟩ text>
|   |     <⟨stack⟩>
|   |       {draw the **card**}
|   |       <⟨stack⟩ direction=«"horizontal"» justify=«"center"»>
|   |         <⟨button⟩ color=«"primary"» onClick={deal a card}>Next card</⟨button⟩>    code({deal a card})
|   |       </⟨stack⟩>
|   |     </⟨stack⟩>
|   |   </⟨container⟩>

⟪## The deck:  four words to start -- add your own⟫

[action to_do_something L0]
| to «add (word as text) meaning (meaning as text)»    ‾value(word as text) ‾value(meaning as text) bold(word) bold(text) bold(meaning) bold(text)
|   add (**a new flash card** with **word** = the **word**, **meaning** = the **meaning**) to **quiz's deck**    ‾value(a new flash card with word = the word, meaning = the meaning) ‾value(quiz's deck)

add _"el gato"_ meaning _"the cat"_    ‾call(add "el gato" meaning "the cat")
add _"el perro"_ meaning _"the dog"_    ‾call(add "el perro" meaning "the dog")
add _"la casa"_ meaning _"the house"_    ‾call(add "la casa" meaning "the house")
add _"el libro"_ meaning _"the book"_    ‾call(add "el libro" meaning "the book")

deal a card    ‾call(deal a card)

⟪## A check⟫
⟦// A new card starts face down, as every two sided view does.⟧

[action to_do_something L0]
| to «test turning a card over»
|   set the **card** to **a new flash card** with **word** _"el gato"_, **meaning** = _"the cat"_    ‾value(the card) ‾value(a new flash card with word "el gato", meaning = "the cat")
|   the **card** is face down should be _yes_    ‾question(the card is face down)
|   turn the **card** over    ‾value(the card)
|   the **card** is face up should be _yes_    ‾question(the card is face up)
