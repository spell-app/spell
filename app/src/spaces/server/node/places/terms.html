Spell's terms

What Spell keeps for you, and how you take it away.

Your data is yours

  • Everything in a space is kept as plain JSON files, one per thing.
  • Any member can download a space whole, as a folder of those files. A program runs on it with one line changed.

Taking your space's id with you

  • Today a space's id (like @family) is Spell's own. It has no keys yet, so there are none to hand over.
  • When Spell moves spaces to AT Protocol's Spaces, each space gets a real AT Protocol id, with keys.
  • Spell promises to hand those keys to a space's admins when they ask, so the space can move to another host with its id.

Deleting

  • A space's admin can delete it. It's removed from Spell at once.
  • Deleting an account removes its own space, and its place in every org.
  • Spell's nightly backups keep a week of copies, so something deleted is gone from them a week later.

Limits

  • One thing may hold about 1 MB, and a space 100 MB.
  • A save over either is refused, and stays on your page, saying why. Nothing is lost.

Abuse

  • Spell takes down what it must not host, and logs who did it and why.
  • To report abuse, or ask about a takedown, write to the address on the sign-in bar.
