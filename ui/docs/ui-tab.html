Tab A tab is a hidden section of content activated by a menu.

A basic tab.

Two tags: each <ui-tab> is a pane of content, and <ui-tabs> draws the row of tabs from its panes' labels. A pane is hidden until it's the selected one; see the Usage tab.

The pane: whatever you put inside the <ui-tab> shows here.

The tabs can be a segmented control: one rounded bar, the selected tab filled with colour.

  • appearance="segmented" turns it on.
  • The fill is the primary colour, or the tabs' color.
  • appearance takes every look the menu has; the words in the examples above (pointing, text ...) are short forms of it.

alignment places the tabs in their row. A segmented tab list moves as a whole.

  • left, center or right: packed at that end
  • fluid: the tabs fill the row

equal makes every tab the same width, worked out from the tabs themselves: no count of tabs to keep up to date.

  • On its own: each tab as wide as the widest one.
  • With alignment="fluid": each tab an equal share of the row.
  • This site's own tabs (Examples, Usage ...) use both, as a segmented control: appearance="segmented" alignment="fluid" equal.

Each <ui-tab> inside a <ui-tabs> is a pane, and its label becomes a tab. No script, and no data-tab paths to pair up.

To have a tab visible on page load, give its pane selected (or set the tabs' value); otherwise the first enabled pane shows. First Second Third

Every <ui-tabs> is its own independent tab group, even nested inside another one's pane.

Not ported: settings to limit a tab group to one part of the page (context, childrenOnly) -- a tab set only ever owns its own <ui-tab> children, so groups never mix. 1A 1B 1C 2A 2B 2C 3A 3B 3C 4 5 6 1A 1B 1C 2A 2B 2C 3A 3B 3C

A lazy pane stamps its <template> into itself the first time it's shown, so hidden panes cost nothing up front. To fetch content instead, listen for ui-opened and fill the pane when event.detail.first is true (see Usage).

Not ported: settings that fetch a pane's content from a URL and cache it (auto, apiSettings, cache) -- a fetch is a few lines in a ui-opened listener, and the pane's loading shows the spinner meanwhile. Tab One
