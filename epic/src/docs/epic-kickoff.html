<epic-kickoff> The <epic-kickoff> element: the box at the top of a new epic's Overview, where you type what the epic should do, pictures and all.

A new epic starts on its page, not in the chat. /epic <name> opens an almost empty epic page, and the kickoff box sits at the top of its Overview: you type what the epic should do, as long as you like, and paste or drop pictures into it.

The page's Send hands it to Claude. It becomes the Kickoff prompt, pictures and all, and the session plans from it, as it plans from a prompt typed after the name in the chat.

For anyone starting an epic.
Status: built (epic epic-engine P75), its own epic-kickoff family.
Its vocabulary: EpicKickoff.en.ts
Its component: EpicKickoff.tsx.

<epic-overview> draws it, in its own shadow root, only on an epic page being reviewed that has no Kickoff prompt and no phases yet. So this page shows it alone, as the Overview would.

  • One box that grows as you type, up to a third of the window.
  • Pictures: paste or drop several at once, or choose them with the small picture button (VS Code's side bar lets no file be dragged in). Each is saved beside the page, in its pictures/ folder, and written into your words on a line of its own, labelled: ![IMG2](pictures/kickoff-...png).
  • A strip under the box shows them, each with its label and an x.
  • The x takes the picture out, and the later labels move down one, in the words that name them too: IMG3 becomes IMG2. Deleting a picture's whole line by hand does the same.
  • Saved as you type, ten seconds after the last key, and at once when a picture comes or goes, the box loses the focus, or the page closes: a kickoff mark in the review inbox.
  • The header's Send hands it over. epic inbox apply writes it as the Kickoff prompt, its pictures labelled, and the box gives way to it.
  • Never written in a page: <epic-overview> draws it.
  • No attributes, slots or events of its own.
