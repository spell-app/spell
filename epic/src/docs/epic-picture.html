<epic-picture> The <epic-picture> element: a picture the person gave an item, a thumbnail with a trash that deletes it and its file.

A picture you paste into the chat while asking for a fix goes onto the issue or todo it becomes. <epic-picture> shows it in the item, after your words: a thumbnail, which a click opens full size.

On an epic page being reviewed, a trash sits at its top right corner. One click deletes it: it goes at once, with Undo in the page's notices for 6 seconds; then the page server takes it out of the item and deletes its file.

For anyone reviewing an epic page.
Status: built (epic epic-engine T87), its own epic-picture family.
Its vocabulary: EpicPicture.en.ts
Its component: EpicPicture.tsx.

An issue filed with a picture pasted in the chat. The trash shows only on an epic page being reviewed: here, the picture alone.

The item above · 4 lines
  • Its picture is the <img> inside it: a page with no script shows it too.
  • A thumbnail, at most 320px wide and 240px tall, its shape kept; a click opens it full size.
  • Its item is the <epic-item> it's written in.
  • Not reviewed: no trash.
  • The trash: one click, no dialog, as a comment's. The picture hides at once; "Picture deleted" shows with Undo for 6 seconds.
  • Then the page server takes it out of the item, writes a line in the Log, and deletes its file. Leaving the page sooner sends the delete at once.
  • Undo puts it back, and nothing changes on disk.
  • The epic tool writes it: epic add <epic>#I "..." --picture <file> (repeatable), and --pasted <n>, the pictures pasted last into the session's chat.
  • The files go beside the page, in pictures/, named for the item and the minute: pictures/i9-20261116-1302.png.
  • It holds one <img src alt>, and has no attributes of its own.
