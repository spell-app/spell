<epic-mark> The <epic-mark> element: an item's id as a chip in its state colour, a link that lands on it.

<epic-mark> draws an item's id as a chip in the item's state colour: <epic-mark item="i126" link> shows I126, red while it waits on Owen, blue while it's under way, green when it's newly decided, grey once it's old. Its tooltip is the item's title; with link, a click lands on the item and opens it.

For anyone writing a phase's To review list, or naming an item in a page.
Status: built (epic epic-engine P45), its own epic-mark family.
Its vocabulary: EpicMark.en.ts
Its component: EpicMark.tsx.

Three items, and a line marking them: each mark takes its item's colour, its title on hover.

The marks above · 1 line
  • The epic tool writes them in a phase's To review field, one per item added while the phase was active and not reviewed yet, with commas between: <epic-field name="to-review"><epic-mark item="j1" link></epic-mark>, ....
  • Before P45 that field held <a href="#j1">J1</a> links, coloured by a script; the migration to-review-marks turns them into marks.
  • It holds no text: the id is drawn from item, in capitals.
