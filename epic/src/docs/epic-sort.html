<epic-sort> The <epic-sort> element: the Epics page's sort buttons, A to Z or newest first, for every list on the page.

<epic-sort> is the Epics page's pair of sort buttons, A–Z and Date: how every <epic-list> on the page sorts the cards in each group.

  • The one in use is pressed, and remembered in this browser.
  • The groups stay where they are: only the cards inside each move.

For anyone changing the Epics page.
Status: built (epic epic-engine P3), in the epic-list family, with <epic-list>, <epic-card> and <epic-sort>.
Its vocabulary: EpicSort.en.ts
Its component: EpicSort.tsx.
