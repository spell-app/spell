<epic-choices-bar>A choice page's bar: the feedback, Do it, and where things stand.

A choice page's bar, last on the page and pinned to the window's bottom: the feedback, then "Do it", which sends every changed box and the feedback to Claude. What's typed is kept in the browser at once and saves itself to the page server as a draft as Owen types.

For anyone writing a page Owen answers on (a details or choice page), or changing this element.
built in epic-choices-bar/ (epic epic-workflow P12).

Live: the real element, drawn by the epics pack this page loads. Off a details page's own address nothing is saved or sent: Send says so.

A part is a piece of what the element draws in its shadow root (its own hidden markup), named so a stylesheet can reach it: epic-choices-bar::part(base).

Part What it is
base The bar.
feedback The feedback box, an <epic-text-box type="feedback">.
send "Do it" (<ui-button>): its tooltip counts the changed boxes.
meta The line under them: the save status (only when it failed), Copy my answers, Expand all.
copy Copy my answers (<ui-button>): what's typed, as the draft's JSON, onto the clipboard.
expand Expand all / Collapse all: every section of rows.
error Why "Do it" failed.
sent What was sent, and when; orange once changed since.

A state is a word the element sets on itself while something is true, for styling: epic-choices-bar:state(stale). Never written in the file.

State When
stale Changed since the answer was sent: press Do it again.
  • Ivory, as the page's toolbar at the top; pinned to the window's bottom while the rows scroll above it.
  • The feedback box grows with its text; "Do it" (a pill with a paper plane) at its right, its tooltip counting the changed boxes.
  • Under them: the save status only when a save failed (red, bold), then Copy my answers and Expand all at the right; then what was sent (green, orange once changed since), or why not (red).

What's typed is never only in the open page (epic epic-engine P15):

  • Every change goes into the browser's storage at once, and to the page server half a second later.
  • A save that fails says why in red, "Kept in this browser; trying again every 3s", and does: once the page server answers again (a restart's new token too), it saves with no keystroke and no reload.
  • On load, the newest of the server's draft, the browser's copy and the answer sent comes back.
  • Copy my answers puts what's typed on the clipboard, as the draft's JSON: for a page whose server is gone for good, where even a reload brings back nothing. Saved as <slug>.draft.json5 beside the page, the page shows it again.
  • epic choices create: in the template.
  • Older pages: the migration step answer-elements.
