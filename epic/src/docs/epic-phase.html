<epic-phase> The <epic-phase> element: one phase of a plan, in the Phases section.

<epic-phase> is one phase of the plan, in the Phases section.

  • Its attributes hold its id, title, status, estimate and what it took; its children are its fields, plan changes and commits.
  • It draws the title line (P2 · Title) with its status icon and the estimate (once done, what it took), and folds.
  • Its plan changes are <epic-update> blocks at the end of its description, with no box around them (Owen, 2026-10-11).

Written for: anyone reading or writing an epic page's markup, or changing this element.
Status: built in epic-phase/.
Its vocabulary (attributes, slots, parts, children): EpicPhase.en.ts.
The component: EpicPhase.tsx.

A page's own buttons go in its title row: <ui-button slot="actions"> children (epic epic-workflow P25).

  • They sit after the title, before its review buttons (while the page is reviewed); the fold chevron is at the row's start.
  • ⌥-click (Alt-click) on its chevron or title, or ⌥+Enter on the focused chevron, opens it and everything in it (sections, phases, items, asides and cards), parts loading as they open; on an open one it folds everything inside, then the block itself. The chevron's tooltip says so. Its title row has no expand-all or collapse-all button of its own (epic epic-engine I161): the page's toolbar keeps one.
  • The pointer over its chevron, status icon, title, time pill and buttons. Over the row's empty space: the grab cursor on a to-do phase while the page is reviewed (it drags there), else the pointer, as a click there folds it.
  • A click on one never folds or unfolds it.
  • The docs runtime puts the block's bullhorn there too, so it never sits over a button.
  • The estimate (2-3h) comes just before the review buttons, one width on every row, so the buttons line up row to row.

Where its children show: a child goes in a slot by its slot attribute.

Slot What goes in it
(default) Its fields, plan updates and commits.
title A title with markup, in place of title.
changes An older doc's plan changes (<epic-updated slot="changes">), until the tool migrates them: drawn at the end of its body, unboxed.
actions Buttons of the page's own in its title row (<ui-button slot="actions">): after the title, before its own buttons; a click on one never folds it. The docs runtime puts the block's bullhorn here too.

  • Its title: always in full, as many lines as it needs, folded or open. Everything in its title row, the plan-changed pen too, sits on the title's first line, so nothing moves as it folds (Owen, 2026-10-19).
  • The status icon, in the colour scheme: its SHAPE by its status (to do, an empty circle; under way, half filled; done, a check), its COLOUR as its Phases chip's, the first that holds (epic epic-engine I170; Owen, 2026-10-29): red, its plan waits for your Accept or a choice in it is open; orange, changed since you looked; blue, under way or worked on; a DOTTED blue circle, scheduled; green, done in the last 3 days (done-at); grey, done before. A done phase's title reads quieter (muted).
  • Claude at work on it: the status icon is a turning spinner, the header's, its tooltip saying whether he's writing its plan or working on it; blue, unless it's red or orange first.
  • The estimate as a badge at the title's end.
  • Plan changes: while it isn't done and its plan changed, an orange pen and the count on its title, folded too. Inside, each change is its own <epic-update> block at the end of its description, with no box around them (Owen, 2026-10-11: "lose the "Plan changes" surround"; before, a Plan changes box atop its body).
  • Its title bar on the row band, edge to edge, sticking under the Phases section's title while you read inside it.
  • Files and Verify stay hidden until a gear shows them: the Phases section's, for every phase, or the phase's own, last in its title row, which wins for it (epic epic-engine I166). Commits stay hidden until its git icon, before its gear, shows them (epic epic-engine P53, J163); the Log lists them all.
  • Folding: a click on the title folds it; on an epic page it starts folded. With source, its body loads from its part file the first time it opens.
  • epic add-phase <epic> "Short Name" --symptom ... --changes ... [--goal ...] [--files ...] [--verify ...] [--estimate 1-2h] [--after N | --before N]: a new phase, todo; a field not given reads TBD. --after 11 makes it P11.1 (then P11.2), right after P11, and renumbers nothing; --before N inserts it as PN and renumbers the rest. With neither, and a Doc Review last, it goes before the Doc Review as a decimal of the phase before it (P14.1), and a Doc Review under way or done is to do again.
  • epic update <epic>#P<N> --symptom ...: sets its fields.
  • epic phase <epic>#P<N> todo|active|done [--done ...]: its status, and its Done field.
  • epic update <epic>#P<N> --estimate "1-2h": its estimate (the Overview's total follows).
  • epic updated <epic>#P<N> "..." [--summary "..."]: an <epic-update at summary> block at the end of its description. The changes count is rewritten by the tool on every edit.
  • epic update <epic>#P6 --title "New title" [--html]: its title (or its title slot, with --html); links that say the old title, and items queued for it, follow.
  • epic commit <epic>#P<N> <sha>, epic commits --backfill: its commits, and commits.
  • Its To review field: rewritten by the tool on every edit.
  • epic split: source and part-ids.
