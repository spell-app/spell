<epic-send-buttons> The <epic-send-buttons> element: Send, the paper plane; with countdown, Auto Send's ring round it.

<epic-send-buttons> is Send: the paper plane. A press sends what Owen marked, typed or commented, at once.

  • With countdown (an epic page's header only), it also sends by itself: AUTO SEND. A ring round the plane drains while he leaves the page alone, 2 minutes by default, and at its end what's unsent goes.
  • The header's gear chooses how long: Immediately, 2 minutes, 5 minutes or Off.
  • Without it (a details page, a guide's comments), or with Auto Send off: the plane is filled blue while something waits, and only a press sends.
  • The wand that turned the countdown on and off is gone for good (epic epic-engine P53).
  • It sends nothing itself: it asks its owner, which sends its own way.

For anyone changing how a page's answers or marks reach Claude.

AUTO SEND, only with countdown. Owen, 2026-10-29: "Send works on a 2-minute no interaction timer. This is integrated into the single send button ... with the countdown ring. Pressing it sends immediately."

  • The choice, in an epic page header's gear (Auto Send):
    • Immediately: each change goes as it's finished, a note once its box loses the focus; no ring
    • 2 minutes, the default
    • 5 minutes
    • Off: only a press sends, and the plane is filled blue while something waits
  • Remembered in this browser for every page (localStorage, spell-epics:auto-send; decision Q6). A new key in P53 (J147): the wand's old keys are ignored, so everyone starts at 2 minutes.
  • It runs only while Auto Send is on and something waits (state="unsent").
  • Every key, click, wheel, scroll or input on the page starts it again, in a same-origin frame too; a pointer only moving doesn't.
  • A note box with the focus and words in it holds the ring full (J144): the wait starts once you leave the box. The plane's tooltip says so then.
  • The time is read off the clock, not counted in ticks (J141): a hidden page, whose timers the browser slows, sends on its next tick or as soon as it's shown again, never minutes late.
  • At its end it asks once (epic-send-saving, auto true), as a press would, and stops until something waits again.
    • It runs whether or not a session listens: with nobody listening, the page server wakes the epic, at most once per epic in 10 minutes, never in airplane mode (Q59, B).
    • An epic page's countdown send asks every revisit now (decision Q7).
    • On a stage button it only sends the marks, never accepts the stage (J143).
  • It's the PAGE's, one SendCountdown, shared with the header's gear: a Send drawn again finds it where it was, its ring too.

    • the stage button's: round its check, inside the pill, green
