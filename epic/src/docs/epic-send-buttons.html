<epic-send-buttons> The <epic-send-buttons> element: Send and its countdown, one group. The paper plane sends; the wand turns countdown mode on and off.

<epic-send-buttons> is Send and its countdown, one pill: the paper plane, then the wand.

  • The plane sends what Owen marked, typed or commented, as every Send did.
  • The wand turns COUNTDOWN MODE on and off. On, what's unsent goes by itself 2 minutes after he stops typing, clicking and scrolling.
  • Off, the plane is filled blue while something waits: he presses it himself.
  • It sends nothing itself: it asks its owner, which sends its own way.

For anyone changing how a page's answers or marks reach Claude.
Status: built (epic epic-engine P20), in the epic-send-buttons family.
Its vocabulary: EpicSendButtons.en.ts
Its component: EpicSendButtons.tsx
The countdown: SendCountdown.

The example above · 5 lines
  • Nothing to send, something waiting, all of it sent.
  • The middle one's ring turns blue, clockwise, while you leave this page alone; any key, click or scroll starts it again.
  • Its 2 minutes running out sends nothing here: nobody on this page listens.
  • A click on a wand turns countdown mode off on every page in this browser: click it again to turn it back on.

Owen, 2026-10-22: "when it is on, you wait for 2 minutes of inactivity from me (I haven't typed / clicked / scrolled) and when that is reached you submit the answers".

  • It runs only while countdown mode is on and something waits (state="unsent").
  • Every key, click, wheel, scroll or input on the page starts it again, in a same-origin frame too.
  • At 2 minutes it asks once (epic-send-saving, auto true), and stops until something waits again.
  • It's the PAGE's, one SendCountdown: a Send drawn again (a docs page's, at every redraw of its comments) finds it where it was, its ring too.
  • The mode is on by default, remembered in this browser for every page (localStorage, spell-epics:send-countdown; decision Q6).
