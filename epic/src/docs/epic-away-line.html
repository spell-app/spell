<epic-away-line> The <epic-away-line> element: one line of "0. While you were out", with its own Read tick.

One line of an epic page's "0. While you were out" (a phase done, a call waiting, an issue, a test), or the heading of one of its parts, with a ✓ Read at its start while the page is reviewed. Read, it fades at once; the next epic inbox apply takes it off the section, and the rest stays.

For anyone reading or writing an epic page's markup, or changing this element.
Its vocabulary: EpicAwayLine.en.ts.
Its component: EpicAwayLine.tsx, with its style sheet and tests beside it.
Since 2026-11-03 (epic epic-engine I206; Owen: "a tick per section as well, so I can say 'that's good' on part but not on the whole thing").

A part's heading, then two lines, as the tool writes them (the ticks show on a page being reviewed):

What was done P7 · Example done: the thing, built (a10023c2a, merged) Still to do 1. I9 open issue: it flickers
  • line: what it's about, a phase's or an item's id (p-x, i205), or a part's (done, todo). Its Read is a Seen on away:<line>.
  • heading: a part's heading; read, its whole part leaves.
  • The section lists its lines' keys in its lines, and the page remembers when each was read in its away-seen: a line read before its last change shows again.

The epic tool, every time it writes the page (PlanDoc.refreshAway()), from NightSummary; never by hand. epic inbox apply applies a Read (PlanDoc.readAwayLine()); Done reading on the section forgets every line's.

Last checked 2026-11-03 against branch epic-engine.
