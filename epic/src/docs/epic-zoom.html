<epic-zoom> The <epic-zoom> element: a screenshot shown full size over the page, Escape to close.

<epic-zoom> shows one image full size, over the whole window: as large as fits, never cropped, on a dark backdrop, its alt text under it. Click a screenshot on an epic page, a details page or a guide, and it opens here. Escape, a click anywhere or its close button close it.

For anyone putting pictures in a page, or changing how they open.
Status: built (epic epic-engine I129), its own epic-zoom family.
Its vocabulary: EpicZoom.en.ts
Its component: EpicZoom.tsx; which images open in it: ImageZoom.

A request's screenshot, a thumbnail: open the request, then click it.

Nobody writes it in a page: write the image, and a click makes the page's one <epic-zoom>, at the end of <body>.

  • Every docs page: the docs runtime listens for clicks on the whole page (wireImageZoom()).
  • A request's thumbnails take their own clicks (<epic-field>), so they open on a page without the runtime too, like this one.

An image in the page itself, shown at least 64px on its longer side, and one of:

  • shown smaller than its own size: a screenshot scaled into the column, a thumbnail
  • in a <figure>
  • in a link to an image: its own file, or <a href="shot.png" target="_blank">

Never one in a link to anything else (it goes there), in a button, or under data-no-zoom. Under the pointer it shows the zoom-in cursor. A click with a modifier key is the link's, as usual.

AttributeWhat it is
srcThe image's address: shown while it's set, removed as it closes.
altThe image's alt text: the caption.

Pieces of what it draws.

PartWhat it is
baseThe <ui-modal>, over the whole window.
imageThe image, as large as fits.
captionIts alt text, under it.
  • A dark backdrop whatever the page's theme (--epic-zoom-backdrop).
  • The image as large as fits the window, less a 24px margin (--epic-zoom-margin) and the caption's line: never cropped, never enlarged past its own size.
  • The close button in the window's top right corner.
