<epic-list> The <epic-list> element: the Epics page's list, every epic's card in five groups, under a row of saga chips.

<epic-list> is the Epics page's list: its <epic-card>s, grouped, sorted and filtered by the list itself.

  • Five groups, each a small heading with how many of its cards show: Favorites, Active, Planning, Urgent, Done.
  • By title in each group, or newest first, as the page's <epic-sort> says.
  • A chip per saga above the groups, then No saga: a click shows only that saga's epics.
  • It never moves its cards: a live update, or the page server's running cards, just join it.

For anyone changing the Epics page, or what epic pages index writes.

What epic pages index writes, shortened · 8 lines
  • Written by epicListHtml(), the cards by group then title: the order it draws them in by default.
  • class="spell-epics": the docs runtime counts its cards on the section's title, and its state chips filter them, as on a saga page's lists.
  • The page server adds the running epics' cards before the end marker (RunningEpics).

group-titleA group's heading: its icon, title and how many show.
  • The chips: a pill each, the toolbar's deeper ivory while shown, outlined while hidden.
  • The groups: one grid of 330px cards, as many to a row as fit; each heading a small eyebrow across the row.
  • A group with no card showing isn't drawn: hidden by a chip, or by the page's state chips.
  • Each card is placed from its shadow root, by a ::slotted() rule and CSS order: nothing in the page changes.
  • epic pages index: the Epics page's list, between its index:start / index:end markers.
  • Before epic epic-engine P3 the page held hand-made groups (div.spell-epic-group) and the docs runtime moved cards between them. epic pages index writes the list anew: no migration step.
