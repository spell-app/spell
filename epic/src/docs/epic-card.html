<epic-card> The <epic-card> element: one epic's card on the Epics page, its state, link, saga, star and last-worked day.

<epic-card> is one epic's card on the Epics page, drawn from what the epic's page says.

  • Its state's mark before its title, by the one rule every list and page uses: in progress, errors, paused, future, done.
  • Its description, its saga's label and its meta line.
  • A star at its top right: a click stars or unstars the epic on the page server.
  • The day it was last worked on at its bottom right, the full date its tooltip.

For anyone changing the Epics page's cards.
