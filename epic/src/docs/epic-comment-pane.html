<epic-comment-pane> The <epic-comment-pane> element: the floating pane Owen writes a new comment in.

The small floating pane Owen writes a NEW comment in: a bullhorn's, or ⌘ / Ctrl I's on selected text. It saves itself as he types; once closed, the comment is a thread.

For anyone changing how comments look or work on a page.
Its vocabulary (every name it uses): EpicCommentPane.en.ts.
Its component: EpicCommentPane.tsx, with its style sheet (.css) and tests beside it.

Click any bullhorn on this page (the masthead's, a section title's): that's this element, opened by the comments' code under it. It's fixed on the screen, so no copy of it sits here.

Its near property, { left, top, bottom }, is the box of what Owen clicked or selected.
