<epic-change> The <epic-change> element: words an answer changed, marked in the text, with the question's folded chip, and a popup on a click.

A change mark: when an answer rewrites the Overview or a phase, the changed or new words show on an orange ground, with a small chip naming the question that changed them. A click on the words opens a popup: which answer changed them and when, the words they replaced, and Reopen, which opens the question again.

For anyone reading or writing an epic page's markup, or changing this element.
Its vocabulary (every name it uses): EpicChange.en.ts.
Its component: EpicChange.tsx, with its style sheet and tests beside it.
Since 2026-10-24 (epic epic-engine P30, Q13 B). The <epic-update> blocks written before stay as they are.

A paragraph with two marks, as an Overview holds them after an answer. The first is quiet now, older than 3 days; the second was accepted on a page from before. Click the words for their popup, a chip to unfold its question and answer.

Caveats and todos were two sections. Now there is one list, Caveats, and todos fold into it, and tests take the letter T.

Live: the real element, drawn by the epics pack this page loads (the main checkout's, until this change merges). On an epic page, the question's title comes from the page too; here there is no Q16, so only the answer shows. Reopen shows only on an epic page being reviewed, while the change is new (3 days).

The rules: PLAN-DOC.md, "Change marks".

what Claude writes, the question's id alone · 3 lines after the tool's next write: stamped, with the words it replaced · 4 lines accepted, by epic inbox apply · 1 line

Its parents: anywhere prose goes, inline, around the words that changed. It's a prose element: an Overview sub-section's text, a phase's fields, an item's text.

Its children: the changed words, running text: links and code too.

  • Mark the words that changed, not the whole paragraph (Q13 chose words over paragraphs, option C).
  • One mark per change; a sentence may hold several, from one answer or from several.
  • Its host (the Overview sub-section, phase or item whose body holds it) lists the marks still waiting, to-accept="ch1 ch3", written by the tool: so the page counts them while the body isn't loaded.

  • Orange: changed since you looked, the one meaning orange has on every doc page (plan-doc.md, "Colours").
  • The words: an orange ground behind the text, wrapping with the line, each line's piece rounded.
  • The chip: small mono capitals in an orange outline; quiet, grey and muted.
  • A click on the words (never a hover; Owen, 2026-10-29) opens the popup under them, its arrow at them: "Changed by Q16 (10/24/26 15:10)", then "Originally: the words it replaced", then Reopen (blue) while the page is reviewed and the change new. A click elsewhere or Escape closes it.
  • The popup is opaque, the page's own colour, its arrow the same: a theme's glass blurs only behind the box, never behind its arrow, which then showed the text under it.
  • Unfolded, the question and answer follow the chip in the line, smaller and muted.
  • The page header counts the changes waiting, an orange label before Send that lands on the next; the stage button's tooltip (Accept Overview, Accept Phases) counts them too.
  • Claude, by hand, as it rewrites the Overview or a phase from an answer: <epic-change item="q16"> around the new words, in a part file or in the HTML it hands a command.
  • epic change <epic> [--for q16] stamps them (id, time, answer, the words they replaced) and lists the ones waiting; any other epic edit stamps them too. --for names the marks written with no item.
  • Reopen is a mark in the page's inbox, under the mark's id; epic inbox apply reopens the question (an old Accept mark makes it accepted).
