<epic-hidden-note> The <epic-hidden-note> element: the line under a filtered list, 3 hidden · show all.

<epic-hidden-note> is the quiet line under a list the state filter hides items of: "3 hidden · show all". A click shows them all again.

For anyone changing how items are filtered.
Status: built (epic epic-engine P3), in the epic-state-filter family, with <epic-state-filter>.
Its vocabulary: EpicHiddenNote.en.ts
Its component: EpicHiddenNote.tsx.

Nobody writes it in a doc: <epic-section> draws it under its items, and the docs runtime under each of the Epics page's lists (wireItemFilters()). With nothing hidden, its owner leaves it out.
