None: it never folds, so it has no open state any more (I301).
  • A plain card (the page's own surface, a thin rule round it): no colour meaning.
  • Its heading, More Details, is in the text's own size and ink, bold, as a Net effect's label.
  • It never folds: it IS the item's text now, so hiding it would hide what the item says.
  • epic details <name>#<id> --more --file html (Add Details, Clarify): the file's HTML goes in the card, REPLACING the item's unclear text. The given "What it says on the tin" body goes for good; the item's own prose moves into its Original Discussion; your quoted words and every card (Choices, Net effect, code, replies, commits) stay. An agent writes it when you click the item's (i) button or Clarify.
  • A second --more replaces the card; the old card's text moves into the Original Discussion, as a rewrite's does
