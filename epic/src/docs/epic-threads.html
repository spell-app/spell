<epic-threads> The <epic-threads> element: the comment threads under one block of a page.

The comment threads under one block of a page, in order, a little apart. The comments' code puts one right after the paragraph, list item or cell holding the comments' quotes, else after their block.

For anyone changing how comments look or work on a page.
Its vocabulary (every name it uses): EpicThreads.en.ts.
Its component: EpicThreads.tsx, with its style sheet (.css) and tests beside it.

Two threads under a paragraph:

Live: the real elements, drawn by the epics pack this page loads.
  • Right after a block, or the paragraph holding a quote; a docs section's first in its body; inside a list item or cell, at its end.
  • Its children: <epic-thread>s.
  • Its edges are the block's it's under (an epic page item's note box; a guide paragraph's measure): the page's sheet, spell-comments.css, sets them.
  • The comments' code marks its own data-spell-added: a live patch steps around it, and it never takes one a page writes, as this page's example.
