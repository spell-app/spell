<epic-actions> The <epic-actions> element: a block's actions row on an epic page, its buttons over the block's top right corner.

<epic-actions> is a block's actions row on an epic page: the one row of a paragraph's, a list's or a table's own controls, a line of no height just before the block, its buttons over the block's top right corner. The comments' bullhorn comes first, then a table's expand button.

For anyone adding a control to a block, or changing where the bullhorns sit.
Status: built (epic epic-engine P45), its own epic-actions family.
Its vocabulary: EpicActions.en.ts
Its component: EpicActions.tsx.

A row with two buttons, before a paragraph, as the docs runtime makes it.

A paragraph of an epic page: its controls sit over its top right corner, on the page's ground, and never push its text aside.

The example above · 5 lines
  • Nobody writes it in a page: the docs runtime makes it the first time a block gets a control (addBlockAction()), and takes it away with its last.
  • On every page: every page that draws one loads the epics pack (the docs runtime, the component sets' layouts).
  • data-spell-added: a live update steps around it.
  • Where the block's top edge lies is the runtime's to measure (the block's own top margin may lie between them): it hands it in as --spell-block-gap, and for a table --spell-block-head and head-row.
