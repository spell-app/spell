<epic-page-note-head> The <epic-page-note-head> element: an old page note's head line.

The head line of an old page note (<spell-note>), written into a guide before comments replaced them: who and when, its first line while folded, its status. A click folds the note.

For anyone changing how comments look or work on a page.
Its vocabulary (every name it uses): EpicPageNoteHead.en.ts.
Its component: EpicPageNoteHead.tsx, with its style sheet (.css) and tests beside it.

Live: the real element. On a guide the docs runtime puts one first in each note, and a click opens the note.
