<epic-send>A details page's Send: where the answer stands, and the round button that sends it.

A details page's Send, in its sticky header: where the answer stands, then Send (<epic-send-buttons>). It sends the whole page, partial answers too, as often as Owen likes: the page never locks. No wand or countdown since 2026-10-25: only a press sends. Beside them, the draft's save: what's typed and not sent is kept beside the page, and comes back when the page reloads or is rewritten. After the button, who hears it: repo-split is listening while a session waits for the answer, else nobody is waiting yet. Send works either way.

For anyone writing a page Owen answers on (a details or choice page), or changing this element.
built in epic-send/ (epic epic-workflow P12; the draft P18; who hears it, epic epic-engine r6).
Its vocabulary (every table below comes from it): EpicSend.en.ts.

Live: the real element, drawn by the epics pack this page loads. Off a details page's own address nothing is saved or sent: Send says so.

details page · the header · 3 lines
  • Once a page, in its sticky header (.spell-page-head), after the h1: it sits at the header's right.

A part is a piece of what the element draws in its shadow root (its own hidden markup), named so a stylesheet can reach it: epic-send::part(base).

Part What it is
base Its box: the line, then an error when there is one.
state Where the answer stands, in its colour: grey, blue (not sent), green (sent).
draft The draft's save (P18), muted: draft saved 14:04, draft from 14:04 put back; when the page server can't take it, on its own line under the rest, red and bold (P15: the browser still keeps it, and it tries again every 3s).
send Send (<epic-send-buttons>, epic epic-engine P20), by the fill rule: a grey outline with nothing to send, dashed blue with changes not sent, a blue outline once sent.
who Who hears a Send, muted, after the button (r6): repo-split is listening with a green dot (a session's epic ask wait), or nobody is waiting yet; the tooltip says more. None where the page can't ask (no page server).
error Why it couldn't send, under them.
notice Sent with nobody waiting, under them in orange: Saved: nobody is waiting yet; Claude reads it when it next looks. Gone once a session waits, or at the next send.

A state is a word the element sets on itself while something is true, for styling: epic-send:state(pending). Never written in the file.

State When
pending Something on the page isn't sent yet.
sent The page as sent.
  • The line: Not sent (grey; blue with something on the page), Changes not sent (blue), Sent 10/8/26 14:34 · 5 of 17 decided (green; Sent 10/8/26 14:34 on a page with no questions, only comment boxes and notes).
  • Send by the fill rule: a grey outline with nothing to send, dashed blue with changes not sent, a blue outline once sent; never solid, since the page can't tell when Claude is done with it.
  • The draft's save beside the line, muted (draft not saved yet, saving draft…, draft saved 14:04, draft from 14:04 put back). Saved to the page server as Owen types, half a second after each change, and kept in the browser at once. Gone once sent.
  • After Send, who hears it, muted (epic epic-engine r6): repo-split is listening with a green dot while a session's epic ask wait (or ask show --wait) waits for the answer, else nobody is waiting yet. The page asks again every 30s: ask wait keeps a heartbeat in ~/.spell-epic/asks/.
  • Send works with nobody waiting too: the answer is saved beside the page, and an orange line says so, Saved: nobody is waiting yet; Claude reads it when it next looks.
  • A draft's save that failed, on its own line under them in red, bold, so the side bar's width never cuts it short (epic epic-engine P15): draft kept in this browser only: ...; trying again every 3s. It does try again, by itself: once the page server answers (a restart's new token too), it saves with no keystroke.
  • Why it couldn't send, in red, under them.
