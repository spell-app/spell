<epic-choices> A set of option cards: side by side while open, folded under Choices with the chosen one marked once answered.

A set of option cards (<epic-option>s): the options a question offers, or the options any item's text weighs. While open it draws the cards side by side; once answered, it folds them under a Choices heading, the chosen one marked.

Its parents:

Anywhere prose goes (it's a flow element), so on any item kind:

  • <epic-item>: a question's own set, last in its text; or the options a judgement call or issue weighs. An item may hold several sets.
  • <epic-reply> and <epic-more>: options a reply or More Details weighs (test V1 of the epic-components epic has a reply ending in one)
  • <epic-version>: as it was, in an Original Discussion
  • <epic-field name="choices">: a phase's own sets, so its forks stay in its story (P50); its picks are keyed by the phase, and its title says "1 choice open" until picked
  • <epic-section kind="choices">: the page's standing sets (standing, each with its own id and title), between the Overview and the Phases (P50)

Its children:

Child How many What it holds
<epic-option> one or more The options, A, B ...
Name Values Meaning Required
chosen text The chosen option's letter, once answered; absent while the question is open. A multiple set's may name several: chosen="A C".
multiple boolean Pick any: several options may be picked. Its heading's switch flips it while reviewed.
fixed boolean No Add choice: Owen may not add options. Without it, every open set takes his.
closed boolean Done: no picks, and no options added, edited or removed; drawn answered.
standing boolean A standing set, in the Choices section: never folds away; each round's picks fold under Picked.
title text A standing set's heading. Its id keys its marks.
chosen-on date The day Owen's pick was recorded (epic inbox apply, epic choose, a revisit's pick kept); shown inside the unfolded Choices, with chosen-note.
chosen-note text What recording the pick did, and what happens next: recorded as the answer; waiting for a phase to take it up. Shown inside the unfolded Choices, so a pick needs no Noted card of its own (I153).

A slot is a place in the drawing that a child goes to: a child with slot="name" goes to that name's place, every other child to the default one (shown as default).

Slot What goes there
default The options.
picked A standing set's picked options (picked-on): folded under Picked.
removed Its removed options (removed), never erased: folded under Removed.

A part is a piece of what the element draws in its shadow root (its own hidden markup), named so a stylesheet can reach it: epic-choices::part(base).

Part What it is
base The cards side by side; answered, the folded Choices aside.
toggle Answered: the Choices <button> that folds the options.
chosen Answered with an option chosen: ✓ Chosen: B · Bananas in the toggle, green, folded or not.
panels Answered: the box of option panels, hidden while folded.
note Answered, with chosen-on or chosen-note: the pick's line atop the panels, Chosen 10/29/26: ....

A state is a word the element sets on itself while something is true, for styling: epic-choices:state(answered). Never written in the file.

State When
answered Its question is answered (chosen, or answered on its item): folded.
open Answered, and its options unfolded.
answering An <epic-ask>'s options (a page Owen answers on): one card a row, each with a tick box.

The colours mean the same on every doc page: the whole scheme is on Epic Page Colours (its source: plan-doc.md, "Colours").

  • Open (no chosen): the cards side by side, as many columns as fit. Each card folds by its header, and starts open.
  • Answered (chosen, or answered on its item): folded under a Choices heading on a warm aside fill; a click on it unfolds the options as panels. The chosen panel has a check before its letter and green text, and is the only one open.
  • While the page is reviewed, each card gets a Choose pill: the fill rule in green, dashed until sent, outlined once sent, solid on the chosen option (the pick applied). See <epic-option>.
  • In an <epic-ask> (epic epic-workflow P12): one card a row, full width, each with its tick box and comment box.
  • Any command taking HTML (add --details, decide --details, details --file): an <epic-choices> is kept; an old option grid (ui-grid.spell-pros-cons, labels A · Title (recommended)) is turned into one on the way in (IncomingHtml)
  • plan-doc decide <name> <Q id> "answer" --option B: sets chosen on the question's set; a question has one answer, so no other set stays chosen
  • epic inbox apply <name>, for Owen's pick: sets chosen on THAT set (picks name a set by its position in the item); a question is answered with the option, any other item approved with it
  • details --file on an answered question keeps the chosen letter when the new options still have it
  • older docs: the converter, from a question's own option grid
