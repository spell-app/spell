<epic-clarify> The <epic-clarify> element: Clarify, and the circle-question beside an item's body, asking Claude to clarify it at once.

An item with only a title says What it says on the tin. where its details would be. <epic-clarify> is the Clarify after those words: while the page is reviewed, a link that asks Claude for the item's details at once, without waiting for Send.

With icon, it's a small circle-question at the right of every item's body, on an epic page being reviewed. A click asks Claude to clarify that text at once; while Claude works on it, the icon turns like a coin.

Either click leaves the page where it is: the link is a button drawn as a word, never a # link, and a session told of the request takes it by itself, so no notice appears at the page's top.

For anyone reviewing an epic page.

The item above · 3 lines

The icon on its own, as <epic-item> draws it beside a body. Not reviewed here, so nothing shows.

The icon · 1 line
  • Its item is of, else the <epic-item> it's written in.
  • A click is the old Add Details: a request for now, posted to the page server at once. The epic's session takes it, or the page server wakes the epic when nobody is listening.
  • The item then shows the request under way, as any request for now; its busy button calls it off.
  • Not reviewed: nothing is drawn.
  • The link: not drawn while the request is on its way or under way.
  • The icon: kept while Claude works on its item's details, turning like a coin, then resting a second; a click then calls the request off. With reduced motion it stands still, in the accent colour.
  • Link and icon are one ask: a given body shows both, and the icon turns while Claude answers either.
  • The epic tool writes it, in the body it gives an item with only a title (PlanItem.giveBody()); the migration item-bodies brings older bodies up to it on the page's next edit.
  • <epic-item> draws the icon itself, in its shadow root, beside its body while the page is reviewed (its part clarify).
  • It holds nothing.
